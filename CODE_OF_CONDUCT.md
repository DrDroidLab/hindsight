# Code of conduct

Hindsight is built by and for engineers who get paged. Treat everyone in issues, pull requests, discussions and any community channel the way you would want a colleague to treat you during an incident: be patient, assume good intent and stay on the problem.

## Expected

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when reviewing someone's first contribution.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Keep real secrets, transcripts and other people's data out of public threads.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling or sustained disruption.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block participants who break these rules. Report a problem privately through the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/DrDroidLab/hindsight/security/advisories/new) or by contacting a maintainer directly; reports are kept confidential.

This code of conduct is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
